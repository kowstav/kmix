Required corrections before implementation:

1. The proposed five moment functions span only three independent functions:
   A_(1-u)=A_0-A_u and A_((1-u)^2)=A_0-2A_u+A_(u^2). Use three independent
   moments or an explicitly defined higher-order/orthogonal basis. Moments
   retain limited order information, not an injective word representation.
2. Fixed glyph features are a linear map of one-hot character features:
   G_k=G A_k. Given complete A_k, they add no information; they may impose
   useful parameter sharing. Measure that inductive bias against an equally
   sized random projection or spelling control. Define/package glyph features
   deterministically; do not add an external font/OCR dependency to decoding.
3. Soft edit energy -tau log(sum_alignment exp(-cost/tau)) can be negative
   and favors words with many alignments. It is not automatically a metric,
   a positive-semidefinite kernel or a normalized edit channel. As a score,
   normalize over candidates and price length bias; as a generative channel,
   use proper pair-HMM transitions, emissions and termination probabilities.
4. The optimal one-candidate boost a*=logit(r)-logit(p) is valid when r and p
   describe the SAME next-event Bernoulli outcome. "Appears somewhere in the
   next256 words" is not the next-word probability. Learn a per-step hazard
   h_d; cumulative recurrence is1-product_d(1-h_d). Charge every nonoccurrence.
5. TopK construction, signed decay, eviction and normalization are not
   automatically O(K). Candidate search, memory scans and full vocabulary
   softmax may dominate. Derive/cache dictionary-only neighbors at startup
   or use bounded indexes, and measure their actual CPU/RAM costs.
6. Adding correlated log-odds may double-count evidence. Train/estimate joint
   gates or compare a clipped finite family. The numerical examples in the
   files are illustrative, not estimates of English word relations.
7. A residual mixer does not guarantee beta_E tends to zero or preserve a
   baseline automatically. Initialize the exact identity, keep a neutral
   branch, and compare against the identical extra stage with empty/random
   inputs. Earlier neutral SX patches already increased native archives.
8. The frozen coder's observed word likelihood does not give probabilities
   on every alternative word. The normalizer for P0(w)exp(eta(w)) cannot be
   reconstructed from observed whole-byte costs. Use a valid additive
   event/tree screen first or collect final BIT probabilities for a locally
   normalized odds correction. Those are distinct models.
9. At a cut slice EOF, charge any partial code/escape prefix without falsely
   declaring a completed word. Audit the old root scorer's truncated-event
   handling before relying on tiny gains. New screens must cover those cases.
10. Dictionary-rank priors, prefix-built graphs and memory cost RAM/CPU/code;
    offline fitted edges, selectors or geometric tables cost package bits
